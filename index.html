Privacy Policy

Astrology

Package ID: kb.astrology · Developer: Kanwar Bhajneek · Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Summary: Astrology does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data. All planetary calculations run offline on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or account information. You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the app.

2. On-Device Data Storage

The app stores only your basic display preferences locally on your device so your settings persist between sessions:

  • Your selected Lagna (Ascendant sign)
  • Your preferred app language (English or Hindi)

This preference data never leaves your device, is not accessible to the developer or any third party, and is permanently deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

3. Offline Astronomical Calculations

All live planetary positions, North Indian Vedic chart renderings, house details, and upcoming transit calculations are computed directly on your device using embedded astronomical algorithms. No personal or birth data is transmitted over the internet.

4. Permissions

Astrology does not request access to your device location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or personal files.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app contains no third-party advertising networks, behavioral trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from any users, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if app features change in the future. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the support email listed on the Astrology app page on the Google Play Store.
